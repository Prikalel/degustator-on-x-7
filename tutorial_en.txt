HOW TO PLAY — DEGUSTATOR ON X-7

GOAL
Clear every level in a single run. Each level is a board you must cross from
START (top-left) to EXIT (bottom-right). The next level unlocks only after you
finish the previous one. Clear the LAST level to win. There are no saves: if you
fail, the whole run restarts from Level 1.

THE BOARD
The path is a serpentine (snake-like) track. Arrows on each row show its flow:
   >  right       <  left       v  down
You can NEVER move back toward the start (no moving up). On right-flowing rows
you go right or drop down; on left-flowing rows you go left or drop down. A cell
where the path splits into two forward options is a BRANCH — you will get to pick.

Cells:
   - a gap / dash  = no cell here, no passage
   - 0 / empty     = a cell you may stand on
   - a number      = an item lying on that cell (its id)

YOUR STATS  (top bar)
   POWER   stamina spent to move. If it drops below 0 -> you collapse = GAME OVER.
   HUNGER  every move costs exactly 1 hunger. Below 0 -> starvation = GAME OVER.
   COINS   money. The ONLY stat carried over from one level to the next.
POWER and HUNGER RESET to the level's starting values at the start of every level.

A TURN
1. EAT PHASE (optional): before AND after a move you may eat items from your
   inventory to restore POWER/HUNGER. Eaten items disappear.
2. CHOOSE A MOVE: pick one die option, e.g. "d9  cost 3". You pay its POWER cost
   (the move also costs 1 HUNGER). Greyed-out options cost more POWER than you have.
3. ROLL: the die rolls 1..its max. You advance that many cells forward.
4. LANDING: if a branch gives several reachable cells, pick one. If the roll
   overshoots the EXIT, you land on the EXIT (level complete). Landing on an
   item's cell auto-picks it into your inventory.
Reach the EXIT to finish the level.

ITEMS & INVENTORY
Inventory is unlimited. Each item shows how much POWER and HUNGER it restores
when eaten, and its sell value. Eat to stay alive — but items you keep until the
end of the level are worth coins.

END OF LEVEL
- POWER and HUNGER reset for the next level.
- ALL leftover inventory is auto-sold for COINS (sell value).
- The SHOP opens: spend coins to BUY items that carry into the next level. Buy
  price can differ from sell value. Can't afford it = you can't buy.
- Only bought items carry over; coins carry over too. Buy nothing and you start
  the next level with an empty inventory.

CONTROLS
Menu:         ENTER/Space or click "Start Run"  ·  T = Tutorial  ·  L = language  ·  ESC = quit
Scroll text:  mouse wheel or Up/Down
Eat phase:    1-9 or click an inventory row to eat  ·  ENTER or click "Done" to continue
Move:         1-n or click a die button
Dice roll:    SPACE to skip the animation
Landing:      1-n or click a highlighted cell
Shop:         1-n or click an item to buy  ·  ENTER or click "Continue"  ·  wheel / Up-Down to scroll
Game over / Victory:  any key or click to return to the menu

TIPS
- Don't waste POWER on a big die if a cheap, small roll already reaches the exit.
- Loot items on Level 1, then BUY food in the shop to survive the harder levels.
- Watch HUNGER: it ticks down on every single move, no exceptions.

Good luck, taster!
